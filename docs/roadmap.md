# BlockParty Protocol Roadmap

BlockParty is architected to be **stable, minimal, and complete**. The protocol is intentionally light, extensible by design, and not driven by a traditional feature roadmap.

This document exists not to outline what’s missing — but to invite exploration.

## 🔍 Why No Roadmap?

The core protocol is the culmination of nearly a decade of iterative thought, testing, and reduction. Its power lies in what’s absent: no hard-coded platform logic, no protocol bloat, no roadmap-driven churn.

Instead, BlockParty encourages an ecosystem of:

- 💡 Extension-defined types
- 🧠 World-specific semantics
- 🔌 Pluggable behaviors
- 🛠️ Independently developed clients

## 🛣️ Areas of Future Exploration

While the protocol itself is complete, others may wish to build or extend in directions like:

- ✍️ New content types (e.g. polls, collaborative blocks, ephemeral reactions)
- 🧩 Namespaced extensions for commerce, games, or creative collaboration
- 🧭 Discovery tools (semantic search, trending indices, world maps)
- 📥 Reference client components for caching, rendering, or offline sync
- 🛰️ Interoperability layers (e.g. ActivityPub, Matrix, IPFS, or blockchain bridges)
- 👥 Social UX experiments: group posts, mutual trust webs, shared feeds
- 🔐 Identity recovery or out-of-band trust signals
- 🧱 Custom blockchains or DAGs built over BlockParty as a substrate

## 🤝 You’re Invited

BlockParty doesn’t evolve by committee or calendar. It evolves because someone, somewhere, needs it to.

If you’ve imagined something worth building on it — you already have permission.
