# 100 Ways to Carry a Block

> *BlockParty isn't a platform. It's a ghost.*
> *A signal that bends, adapts, and survives.*
> *If it can move, it can live.*

BlockParty blocks are not bound by server, socket, or sync. They are portable packets of signed intent that can be transmitted in any medium capable of storing or moving bytes. Whether the network is alive, dead, hostile, or just inconvenient, the block persists.

Here are 100 ways to carry a block.
