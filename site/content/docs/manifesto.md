# BlockParty Manifesto: You Can’t Stop the Signal

We didn’t build BlockParty to disrupt.  
We built it because something was missing.

The internet remembers everything. It assigns you an identity you can't change, a past you can't escape, and a trail you can't erase. It treats people like platforms. It trades truth for permanence.

BlockParty is different.

It is not a website. It is not a product.  
It is a protocol — a quiet, resilient protocol for those who wish to speak, remember, forget, and associate freely, on their own terms.

We believe:

- Privacy is not a luxury. It is a right.
- Identity should be owned, not assigned.
- Friendship should be mutual, encrypted, and deniable.
- Memory should be earned, not extracted.
- Forgetting should be as natural as remembering.
- **Association is sovereignty. Worlds are free to live, fork, and evolve without permission.**

In BlockParty, every action is a block. Every block is signed. Some are encrypted. Most are ephemeral.  
Your presence is your persistence.  
Your voice echoes only as long as others choose to carry it.

There are no servers. No central authority. No \"delete\" button — because nothing is permanent unless someone chooses to remember it.

You can publish. You can connect. You can walk away.  
You can burn your identity — and with it, all the certainty anyone ever had about your past.

This is the Post-Truth State.  
A place where no one is imprisoned by their worst day.  
Where communities can remember differently, associate freely, and shape new worlds without permission or consensus.

BlockParty is latency-tolerant. Planet-ready. Built to run on Earth or Mars, by radio, sneaker-net, QR code, or whispered word.  
It works when networks fail. It lives when platforms die.

We release it to the public domain.  
We make no claim of ownership.  
If it resonates with you, take it. Fork it. Build it. Burn it. Replant it.

We are blessed to be part of its beginning.  
We don’t care who carries it forward — only that it lives.

You can’t stop the signal.  
But now, you can choose how long it echoes.
